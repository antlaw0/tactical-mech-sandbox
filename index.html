Tactical Mech Sandbox

Prototype 0.5 · Audio-first tactical combat

Mission: Scrap Yard

Destroy three hostile mechs. Each friendly mech can move up to four squares and fire twice per round. Enemy turns resolve when you end the round. Obstacles and other mechs block movement and firing lines; adjacent obstacles provide cover.

Battlefield

Blue circles: friendly mechs. Red diamonds: hostile mechs. B: concrete barrier. R: rock. S: scrap barricade. Yellow outline: active friendly mech. Teal outline: selected target. Orange outline: line-of-sight blocker. Rows A to J, columns 1 to 10. Dashed outline: exploration cursor.

Explore battlefield

On iPhone, drag a finger across the battlefield to hear each square. Double-tap a mech to command it or select it as a target. Double-tap reachable ground to select a destination, then confirm with Move mech to cursor. Arrow keys explore without moving your mech. Enter selects a unit. Home returns to your active mech. With a screen reader, use focus mode if arrows are intercepted. You can also use the directional buttons below.
